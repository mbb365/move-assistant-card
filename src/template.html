Connect Apple Health

The Home Assistant app on your iPhone can read Apple Health and send it to Home Assistant. Steps include your Apple Watch.

  1. Update the Home Assistant app On your iPhone, update Home Assistant from the App Store. Apple Health needs version 2026.9 or newer.
  2. Turn on Apple Health In the app: Settings → Companion app → Sensors → Apple Health. Switch it on.
  3. Allow access When iPhone asks, allow Home Assistant to read Steps. Turn on the Steps sensor in the Apple Health list.
  4. Pick it in Move Assistant Settings → Integrations → Steps → choose … Health steps.
When it updates Your phone sends new numbers when the app syncs, for example when you unlock your phone or open Home Assistant. It isn't live to the second.
Not seeing it? Check iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Home Assistant and make sure Steps is allowed.
